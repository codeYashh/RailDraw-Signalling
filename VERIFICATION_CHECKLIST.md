# Indian Railways Signal & Telecom (S&T) Drawing Office
## Standard Symbol Verification Checklist & Registry
**Document Ref:** IR-S&T-CAD-SYM-REG-2026-v1  
**Compliance Standard:** Indian Railways Signal Engineering Manual (IRSEM) Part II & RDSO Standard Drawings

---

### 1. Verification Protocol
In accordance with Railway Board and RDSO drafting instructions, all symbols seeded into the **RailDraw Signalling** CAD engine are initially tagged with status **`NEEDS VERIFICATION`**. 

An authorized S&T officer (**SSE / Drawing**, **ASTE**, **DSTE**, or **Sr. DSTE**) must inspect each symbol against the relevant RDSO standard drawing before marking it as **`VERIFIED`** in the in-app **Symbol Manager**. Once verified, the timestamp, officer designation, and verification note are permanently stamped into the manifest.

### 3. Step-by-Step Verification Procedure in RailDraw
1. Log in or switch to the **`Admin`** role using the top right role switcher.
2. Click on the **`Symbol Library`** button in the header navigation.
3. Click on any symbol marked with **`NEEDS VERIFICATION`** (amber badge).
4. Review the SVG geometry, anchor points, and dimensions against your division's RDSO standard drawing book.
5. Click **`Verify against Official Source`**.
6. Enter:
   - **Verifying Official**: Your name and designation (e.g., `Sh. V. K. Gupta, SSE/Drawing/Delhi`).
   - **Notes**: Reference number of the RDSO correction slip or approved plan (e.g., `Verified as per RDSO SD-10021 Rev 4`).
7. Click **Confirm Verification**. The symbol is immediately updated to **`VERIFIED`** (green badge) across all generated drawings without touching any source code.
